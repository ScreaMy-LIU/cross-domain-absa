Sentence-level Transformer Baseline
===================================

Model:
distilbert-base-uncased-finetuned-sst-2-english

Dataset:
data/sample_absa.csv

Samples: 14

Accuracy: 64.29%

Comparison:
Rule-based baseline: 57.14%
Transformer baseline: 64.29%

Limitations:
- The model is trained for sentence-level sentiment classification.
- It is not specifically trained for Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis.
- It only supports positive and negative labels.
- Neutral sentiment is not explicitly modeled.
- Providing the aspect in the input does not guarantee aspect-aware reasoning.
