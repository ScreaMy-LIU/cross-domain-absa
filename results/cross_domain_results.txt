Cross-Domain ABSA Experiment
============================

Source Domain:
Laptop

Target Domain:
Restaurant

Model:
DistilBERT trained on Laptop ABSA

Evaluation:
Restaurant held-out sentence-grouped test set

Test samples:
738

Cross-Domain Results:
- Accuracy: 69.11%
- Macro F1: 0.5217
- Test Loss: 0.7557

Per-Class F1:
- Negative: 0.5505
- Neutral: 0.1928
- Positive: 0.8220

Domain Comparison:
- Laptop -> Laptop Accuracy: 73.58%
- Laptop -> Restaurant Accuracy: 69.11%
- Accuracy change: -4.47 percentage points

- Laptop -> Laptop Macro F1: 0.6465
- Laptop -> Restaurant Macro F1: 0.5217
- Macro F1 change: -0.1248

Observation:
Performance decreases when the Laptop-trained model is
evaluated on the Restaurant domain.

The decrease is particularly visible in Macro F1.
Neutral sentiment shows the largest difficulty under
cross-domain transfer.
